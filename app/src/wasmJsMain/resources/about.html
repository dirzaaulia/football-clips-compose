About Football Highlights & Clips

Published by Dirza Aulia • Updated for the 2026/2027 European Football Season

Our Mission

Football Highlights & Clips (FC) was built with a single objective: to give football enthusiasts around the world a fast, clean, and reliable hub to watch official match highlights, track full-time scores, and review matchday fixtures without clickbait, fake simulator videos, or invasive spam.

Every matchday weekend, millions of football fans search across video platforms to catch up on European league matches. Unfortunately, the internet is flooded with misleading video game simulations (PES, eFootball, EA Sports FC), fan reaction vlogs, and deceptive reuploads. Football Highlights & Clips solves this by applying strict automated verification and channel whitelisting to curate only legitimate, high-definition match recaps directly from verified leagues and official club media.

Platform Architecture & Technology

Football Highlights & Clips is developed using cutting-edge multiplatform technologies to ensure instantaneous performance across mobile devices and modern desktop web browsers:

🚀 Kotlin Multiplatform & WASM

Built with Jetpack Compose and Compose Multiplatform for WebAssembly (WASM), delivering fluid 60fps native-grade rendering in the browser.

📺 YouTube API Compliance

All video content is embedded through the official YouTube IFrame Player API, guaranteeing full respect for copyright holders and content creators.

⚡ Real-Time Data Pipeline

Integrated with licensed football data feeds and Supabase to deliver fast score updates, schedule notices, and official standings.

🤖 Multi-Layer Verification

Every candidate video is filtered through duration boundaries, audio-visual aspect ratios, and AI verification to eliminate misleading clips.

Commitment to Copyright & Legitimate Broadcasters

Strict Adherence to YouTube Developer Policies

Football Highlights & Clips does not host, rip, stream, or re-encode copyrighted audiovisual material on its servers. All highlights are delivered via standard embed players from official rightsholders including official league channels (such as Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, and Bundesliga broadcasters) and verified club media channels (Real Madrid, Arsenal, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, etc.).

Content creators and official broadcasters retain 100% of their video views, analytics, and YouTube monetized ad revenues from embeds displayed within our platform.
