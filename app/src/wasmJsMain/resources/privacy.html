Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026 • Effective date: September 5, 2026

Welcome to Football Highlights & Clips (also referred to as Football Highlights & Clips or FC, "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Dirza Aulia. Football Highlights & Clips is available across Android and Web platforms (fc.dirzaaulia.com) to provide football video highlights, match scores, and schedules.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our service collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect the absolute minimum amount of data required to deliver a seamless experience across your devices:

  • Account & Sign-In Data: If you choose to sign in with Google (via Google Credential Manager or OAuth), we receive basic public profile information (such as your email address, display name, and unique user ID) provided by Google. This data is handled securely via Supabase Auth. Signing in is strictly used to link and sync your lifetime or active Premium ("Remove Ads") purchase across multiple devices.
  • Purchase Data: When you purchase "Remove Ads" via Google Play Billing or our Web platform, transaction and receipt validation data are processed securely via RevenueCat. We do not store or process your credit card or financial details directly.
  • Device & Usage Data: When running the app, basic non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as OS version, device model, and crash logs) may be gathered via Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics to help us troubleshoot errors and improve performance.

2. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Our application integrates with reputable third-party service providers who may collect information used to identify your device:

📺 YouTube API Services

Match highlights and clips in Football Highlights & Clips are embedded using official YouTube Player APIs from verified broadcaster channels. By using Football Highlights & Clips, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

📢 Advertising (Google AdMob & Google AdSense)

We use Google AdMob (on mobile) and Google AdSense (on web) to display advertisements. Google may use advertising IDs and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.

💳 In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Google Play)

We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and validate receipts for digital purchases. For more information, consult RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

🛡️ Backend & Authentication (Supabase)

User sessions and entitlement verification are securely powered by Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. View the Supabase Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Provide and maintain the core features of Football Highlights & Clips (FC).
  • Verify, restore, and synchronize your Premium ("Remove Ads") status across devices.
  • Monitor stability, diagnose bugs, and resolve application crashes.
  • Serve relevant advertisements to non-premium users in accordance with Google's advertising policies.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain your account identifier only for as long as needed to maintain your purchase entitlements. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated profile data at any time.

To request data deletion, please contact us at dirzaaulia@gmail.com. Upon receiving your request, your account data will be permanently removed from our databases within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Football Highlights & Clips is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
